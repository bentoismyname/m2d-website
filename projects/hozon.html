Hozon

Minimalism does not mean 'nothing'. Uncluttered minimalism celebrates the elegance found in simplicity, creating environments that please the eye and provide respite from the chaos of the modern world.

120B Rivervale Drive · 2023
(Residential · HDB)

Based on our client's wishes, we used light colours to evoke understated minimalism and elegance through the furnishings. Natural light plays a pivotal role, enhancing the openness of the space and creating a seamless connection between the interior and its surroundings.

This design ethos extends beyond the physical, influencing lifestyle choices and fostering a mindset of intentional living. Stripping away excess embellishment embraces 'less is more', creating spaces that exude tranquillity and clarity.

Even the bathroom was not spared. Thoughtful organisation, clean lines and storage solutions ensure that each item has its designated place, promoting both order and efficiency.
