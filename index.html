Do you offer virtual interior design consultations in Singapore?

Yes. We offer virtual design consultations for clients who prefer remote interactions or are located outside our service area. Our team will work closely with you to understand your vision and preferences, providing personalised design solutions tailored to your needs.

Do you handle renovation and construction projects?

Absolutely. We specialise in both interior design and renovation / construction. Whether you're refreshing your space with a new design or undertaking a full renovation, our experienced in-house team brings your vision to life.

How long does an interior design & renovation project take?

Design projects under 1,500 sqft take 3–4 weeks; over 1,500 sqft, 6–8 weeks. Renovation of new units (EC, new condo, BTO with OCS) takes about 5 weeks for 400–700 sqft and 6 weeks for 700–1,000 sqft. Older HDBs, condos and landed homes take 6 weeks (400–700 sqft), 8 weeks (700–1,000 sqft) or 12 weeks (1,000–1,400 sqft). These are estimates and vary with scope and material lead times.

Can you work within my home renovation budget?

Certainly. Your renovation need not cost an arm and a leg. We believe in practical, innovative and affordable interior design, with flexible solutions and transparent pricing so your project stays within budget without compromising on quality.

Are your interior design services licensed in Singapore?

With over 30 years of professional renovation experience, M2 Decor Pte Ltd is a BCA and HDB licensed contractor and an RCMA accredited renovation firm.
