Our design philosophy

Where it all started

M2D's story begins at a carpentry bench. Benjamin's father built the business on woodwork: cabinets, built-ins and the quiet discipline of making things that last. Rooted in the artistry of crafting wood, that background still shapes how we take on every project today.

Father & son

The birth of craftsmanship

Two generations in one workshop. The first ran the fabrication and the crews; the second grew up on site, learned the trade from the ground up and brought design into the conversation. The company name honours that lineage: M Square Decor, the renovation and carpentry arm, and M2D Interior, the design studio.

2015

Changing homes, changing lives

Since 2015 we have focused on Singapore residential design and renovation, treating each home like a consultation rather than a sale. Over 200 households later, we are a trusted design-and-build firm with in-house carpentry, M&E and masonry, and a CaseTrust, HDB and BCA accredited team.

Today

One team, four divisions

M2D has grown into four divisions under one roof: Interior Design & Consultancy, Renovation & Carpentry, the Interior Design Workshop and Handyman & Home Repair. Different colours, one standard of work.

The process

Four stages,
one team.

01

Initial consultation

  • Schedule a consultation through any of our channels
  • Articulate your vision, lifestyle and the atmosphere you want
  • Share specific requirements: budget, timeline, must-haves
02

Concept development

  • We present initial design concepts and a detailed estimated budget
  • Collaborate closely with us: feedback and insights refine the design
  • Align design and budget with your expectations
03

Detailed planning

  • 3D visualisation and 3D video walkthrough
  • Detailed working plan for Stage 1 (foundation)
  • Detailed fitting drawings for Stage 2 (fitting)
04

Execution

  • Foundation: M&E, ceiling, masonry / tiling, plastering, painting
  • Fitting: carpentry, painting, installations
  • Finishing: handover with touch-ups and finishes
FAQ

Questions
we get asked.

Are you an interior design firm or a contractor?

We are an interior design firm with in-house capabilities: carpentry, ceiling and partition works, electrical works and simple masonry. We believe in a streamlined renovation where we both visualise and conceptualise your space, all in one. Covid-19 was a great reminder that with our own capabilities we can keep renovating cautiously without being too affected by restrictions and manpower.

How does M2D differ from other interior design companies?

We pride ourselves as doctors for interior spaces: you share your pain points, lifestyle, wants and needs, and we advise and operate on the apartment based on what you have shared. We are also a one-stop renovation company with both design and in-house build capabilities.

How many years of experience do you have?

Rooted in the artistry of crafting wood, our background in carpentry started back in 2001. Having seen how the industry transformed, we are determined to set the benchmark for what an interior designer should be, making it impossible for black sheep who focus on selling rather than solving.

What is a typical renovation duration?

For a full whole-house renovation above 800 sqft, the typical timeframe is roughly 12–14 weeks depending on the materials and design proposed. Specially indented materials may add shipping time.

What is your payment structure?

As a CaseTrust accredited firm, payment is broken into four phases: 10% at confirmation and design, 40% at foundation stage, 40% at fitting stage and 10% at handover and finishing. Any variation works within the agreed scope are added or deducted accordingly.

Can we see your workmanship in person?

We do not believe in a showroom. We are more than happy to arrange a site visit to a completed or in-progress home, subject to our client's agreement.
